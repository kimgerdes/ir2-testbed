# Demo queries (SciFact, BM25 and bge-small vs LLM2Vec Qwen2.5-0.5B)

`python demo.py` shows the top 5 of the three systems side by side, and the rank of the labelled abstract. The documents are encoded before the demo, only the claim is encoded live (0.06 s on the Mac).

- **Hero**: "0-dimensional biomaterials show inductive properties." The labelled abstract, *New opportunities: the use of nanotechnologies to manipulate and track stem cells*, shares no word stem with the claim. BM25 puts it at rank 1745, bge-small at rank 13 and LLM2Vec at rank 2. The model has to know that "0-dimensional biomaterials" are nanoparticles.
- **Failure**: "Insulin increases risk of severe kidney failure." Every word of the claim is in the labelled abstract, *Diabetes treatments and risk of amputation, blindness, severe kidney failure, hyperglycaemia, and hypoglycaemia*. BM25 and bge-small put it first, LLM2Vec at rank 77 (79 in the testbed run). It returns papers about kidney disease: the abstract is about many diabetes treatments and outcomes, and its mean vector keeps this main topic, not one item of the list. This is our typical loss (see `analysis.ipynb`): many shared words, where BM25 is very strong. The 1.5B model puts it at rank 7.
- **Wildcard**: proposed on the spot. Backups:
  1. "Individuals with low serum vitamin D concentrations have increased risk of multiple sclerosis." (query 623): BM25 rank 11, bge and LLM2Vec rank 3.
  2. Our rewording without the key words: "People who get little sunshine are more likely to develop MS." Both dense models put the same abstract first, BM25 puts it at rank 1742 (only "develop" is shared). The demo shows the labelled abstract of query 623 for it.
  3. "The single flash-evoked ERG b-wave is generated by activity of ON-bipolar cells." (query 1272): BM25 rank 9, bge rank 20, LLM2Vec rank 2.
